# Quantum Spain and CESGA
Building HPC tools for scalable, distributed quantum computing.

🚀 **About Us**  
Quantum Spain aims to promote and finance a competitive and complete quantum computing infrastructure in Spain. Specifically, the CESGA group aims to develop competitive tools which seek innovation in the modern era of synergy between quantum and HPC.

🤝 **Collaborate With Us**  
We are eager to partner with researchers, developers and industry members in order to improve our software. Our aim is to create effective and practical tools in the Spanish quantum ecosystem. Feel free to reach out—we’re excited to connect and explore collaboration opportunities!
